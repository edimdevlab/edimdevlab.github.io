Privacy Policy for SnapCompress

Last updated: August 28, 2026

SnapCompress ("the App") is developed and operated by Edim Dev Lab ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our mobile application.

1. Image and File Processing (100% Local)

SnapCompress does NOT upload, store, or transmit your photos, images, or files to any external server or cloud service. All compression, resizing, and format conversions are performed locally on your device hardware. Your media files never leave your device.

2. Information Collected Automatically

We do not collect personally identifiable information (such as your name, address, or phone number). However, the App uses third-party services that may collect diagnostic and identifier data to serve advertisements and process in-app purchases:

  • Google AdMob: Used to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads. AdMob may automatically collect Advertising IDs (such as Android Advertising ID), IP addresses, diagnostic device data, and interaction information. Learn more at Google Privacy & Terms.
  • Google Play In-App Billing: Used to process one-time in-app purchases (e.g., removing ads). Transaction processing is handled securely by Google Play. We do not receive or store your financial or payment card details.

3. Permissions

The App utilizes the Android Photo Picker to allow you to select images. The App does not require broad storage access permissions (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE or READ_MEDIA_IMAGES) and only accesses the specific images you explicitly choose to compress.

4. GDPR, UK, and US Privacy Rights

Depending on your location (such as the EEA, UK, or specific US states like California), you have rights regarding your personal data and targeted advertising preferences. You can adjust your consent choices at any time via the consent management options presented in the App or directly in your device's Google Ads settings.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
