Privacy Policy for SnapCompress

Effective Date: August 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Edim Dev Lab ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and discloses information when you use the SnapCompress mobile application (the "App").

1. Image and File Processing (Local & Offline)

SnapCompress is designed as a privacy-first utility application. All image compression, resizing, and format conversions are performed entirely locally on your device. We do not upload, collect, store, or transmit your photos, images, or documents to any external servers.

2. Data Collection and Permissions

  • Storage / Media Permissions: The App requests access to read and save media files strictly for the purpose of picking images from your gallery and saving compressed outputs back to your storage.
  • No Personal Data: We do not collect personally identifiable information such as names, physical addresses, or phone numbers.

3. Third-Party Services and Advertising

The App uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify your device for analytics and advertising purposes. These service providers include:

  • Google AdMob: To display banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads. AdMob may collect and process device identifiers, IP addresses, and interaction data to serve personalized or non-personalized advertisements. For more details, please review Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play In-App Billing: To process in-app purchases (such as "Remove Ads"). Payment transactions are securely handled directly by Google Play, and we do not store your credit card or billing details.

4. Children’s Privacy

Our App is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
